Biography

Recognized as the 'Father of Impressionism', Claude Monet (1840 - 1926) instigated one of the most important artistic movements in history, and his work continues to inspire artists today.

Early Life

Claude Monet was born in Paris, France. He began drawing as a boy in Normandy, creating caricatures of his teachers and neighbors. He later studied under landscape artist Eugene Bodin.

Impressionism

Monet painted outdoors to capture changing light and nature's evanescence. Impressionism became possible thanks to paint in tubes, allowing artists to work outside without a studio.

Artistic Techniques

  • Painting outdoors (en plein air)
  • Capturing changing light and color
  • Visible brushstrokes
  • Layering paint for texture

Famous Paintings

  2. Haystacks
  3. Rouen Cathedral
  4. Houses of Parliament
  5. Japanese Bridge
