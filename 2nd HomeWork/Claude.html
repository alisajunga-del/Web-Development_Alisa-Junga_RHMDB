Claude Monet Biography

Recognized as the 'Father of Impressionism', Claude Monet (1840 - 1926) instigated one of the most important artistic movements in history, and his groundbreaking work continues to inspire the development of artists and artistic styles today.

How do artists represent and reflect the world they live in? The fact that the natural world is constantly changing makes depicting it a challenge when it comes to oil paintings, which are static and cannot show motion like movies do. To address this issue, the painter Claude Monet became one of the founders of the Impressionism art movement.

Learn more about Claude-Monet.

Monet’s Artistic Techniques

  • Painting outdoors (en plein air)
  • Capturing changing light and color
  • Using visible brushstrokes
  • Layering paint for texture

Famous Paintings

Water Lilies, Haystacks, Rouen Cathedral, Houses of Parliament, Japanese Bridge
