Claude Monet "The richness I achieve comes from nature, the source of my inspiration."

Biography

Recognized as the 'Father of Impressionism', Claude Monet (1840 - 1926) instigated one of the most important artistic movements in history.

Early Life

Claude Monet was born in Paris. He began drawing as a boy and studied under landscape artist Eugene Bodin.

Impressionism

Monet painted outdoors to capture changing light and color. Impressionism was made possible thanks to paint in tubes.

Artistic Techniques

  • Painting outdoors (en plein air)
  • Capturing changing light and color
  • Visible brushstrokes
  • Layering paint for texture

Famous Paintings

  1. Water Lilies
  2. Haystacks
  3. Rouen Cathedral
  4. Houses of Parliament
  5. Japanese Bridge
