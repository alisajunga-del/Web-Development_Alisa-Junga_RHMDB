Claude Monet "The richness I achieve comes from nature, the source of my inspiration."

Biography

Recognized as the 'Father of Impressionism', Claude Monet (1840 - 1926) instigated one of the most important artistic movements in history, and his groundbreaking work continues to inspire artists and styles today.

Artistic Techniques

  • Painting outdoors (en plein air)
  • Capturing changing light and color
  • Using visible brushstrokes
  • Layering paint for texture

Famous Paintings

Water Lilies, Haystacks, Rouen Cathedral, Houses of Parliament, Japanese Bridge
